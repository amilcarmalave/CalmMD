Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

CalmMD doesn't collect anything about you. There's no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no tracking. Your documents are plain files on your own computer, and they never leave it unless you send them somewhere yourself.

What the app connects to

  • Supporter key activation. Only if you become a Supporter: when you enter your key, the app sends the key and a device label (such as “Windows PC”) to Lemon Squeezy's licence service to activate it, and checks it again about once a month. Nothing else is sent.
  • Things in your documents. Images from the web and video embeds load from the sites they point to, like in a web browser. Video embeds only load when you click them, and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain.

Purchases

Lemon Squeezy processes orders as the merchant of record. They receive the details you enter at checkout (such as your name, email address and payment information) under their privacy policy. We receive the order details needed to support your licence (your email address and order number), and nothing about your payment method.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: GitHub Issues (please don't post order numbers or keys there).
