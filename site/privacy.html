Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

CalmMD doesn't collect anything about you. There's no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no tracking. Your documents are plain files on your own computer, and they never leave it unless you send them somewhere yourself.

What the app connects to

CalmMD itself never contacts a server. The only things that can reach the internet are the things in your documents:

  • Images from the web load from the sites they point to, as in a web browser.
  • Video embeds load only when you click them, and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain.
  • Links open in your browser when you follow them.

Your data

Settings, drafts of unsaved documents and file history are kept in the app's own folder on your computer. Uninstalling CalmMD and deleting that folder removes them.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. It asks GitHub for the latest release to link the right download, and GitHub Pages may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
