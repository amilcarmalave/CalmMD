Questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There isn't one. CalmMD is open-source software under the MIT licence, built by one person who wanted a better place to write. No ads, no paid tier, no data collection. If you'd like to help, star the project, tell a friend, or report a bug.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

CalmMD isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. Click More info → Run anyway. Installing from the terminal (below the download buttons) avoids the message.

macOS says the app “can't be opened”

Right-click CalmMD in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. On recent macOS, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Installing from the terminal avoids it.

How do I sync my notes between computers?

Keep your notes folder in Dropbox, iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive. CalmMD works with plain files and notices when they change on disk.

Does it collect anything about me?

No. No telemetry, no analytics, no account. CalmMD works fully offline. See the privacy page.

Will my files look the same on GitHub?

Yes. CalmMD writes standard GitHub-flavoured Markdown. Extras like highlights and colours fall back gracefully elsewhere.
